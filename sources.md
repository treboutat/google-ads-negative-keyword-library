# Sources & basis

This skill is the public version of TNT Growth's approved starter-negatives method (Starter Negative Keyword Library book, release `tnt-google-ads-2026-09-07.1`): building an initial negative library from business context before search-term data exists.

## The approach
Start from the searches worth protecting, build candidates from specific intent mismatches, choose each match type from the searches it actually blocks, place each exclusion at a deliberate scope, and check every candidate against a blocked and a protected example. Categories are investigation prompts; the skill doesn't ship a universal block list.

## Platform facts
Matching behavior, account-level and shared-list scope, brand controls, Performance Max and Demand Gen limits, and Google Ads Editor import defaults link to Google's documentation and were checked in September 2026. Re-verify before relying on one.

## Relationship to search-term-scorer
- **negative-library-starter** (this skill): *proactive*. Build a starter library from the business when there's no data yet.
- **search-term-scorer**: *reactive*. Review real search terms for relevance, economics and confidence, and maintain the library.

## What this skill deliberately does not claim
- No client names or client results. Examples are hypothetical.
- No search volumes, savings figures or target library size.
- It proposes candidates for human review. Nothing is applied automatically.
